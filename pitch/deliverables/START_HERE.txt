BIDGENIUS AI — YOUR HACKATHON PRESENTATION

1. Open BidGenius_AI_Hackathon.pptx in PowerPoint.
   The five-minute presentation has 10 main slides.
   Three extra Q&A slides are hidden after the closing slide.
   Each slide includes its script, timing and delivery cues in Notes.

2. Rehearse with BidGenius_AI_Presenter_Guide.pdf.
   Read the script paragraphs aloud; do not read the cue labels or directions.
   The guide also includes a shorter three-minute script and judge Q&A.
   Use its first page as a printed cue sheet.

3. Use BidGenius_AI_Hackathon.pdf as a ten-slide fallback.
   The PowerPoint contains the editable slide text and shapes.
   Product screenshots are embedded, so the deck needs no internet connection.

The screenshots show the prepared 10 October 2026 demo snapshot.
The saved application may contain later changes and different figures.
The default presentation uses screenshots and does not require a live demo.

Rebuild from the project root:
  .venv\Scripts\python.exe pitch\prepare_pitch_assets.py
  node pitch\build_hackathon.mjs
  cscript.exe //Nologo pitch\render_native.vbs
  node pitch\build_presenter_guide.mjs

The existing pitch/BidGenius_AI_Pitch.pptx and application files were preserved.
